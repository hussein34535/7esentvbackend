# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hussein (owner/operator) and content admins of **7esen TV** — a sports streaming service. They manage live TV channels, match listings, full-match replays, goals/highlights clips, subscription packages, coupons, payments, and user accounts. They work long sessions daily, often on laptop, sometimes phone. Speed and clarity matter more than decoration.

## Product Purpose

Admin panel (Next.js App Router) that powers the 7esen TV mobile app backend: CRUD for content (channels, categories, matches, news/full-matches, goals, highlights), commerce (packages, coupons, payments), moderation (requests, users), and analytics. Success = an operator can find, add, edit, publish content fast without hunting for controls.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, effortless. Soft like iOS — gentle surfaces, quiet depth, nothing shouting. Professional tool that disappears into the task.

## Anti-references

- Pure black dark mode / terminal aesthetics — explicitly rejected by the owner.
- Stark pure-white clinical dashboards — also rejected.
- Loud gradients, neon glow, glassmorphism-everywhere, AI-slop hero sections.
- Dense enterprise SAP-style grids with zero breathing room.

## Design Principles

1. **Soft, not flat; light, not stark.** Tinted cool-gray surfaces, hairlines instead of heavy borders, depth from subtle layered shadows.
2. **One accent earns its place.** Emerald (brand green) marks primary actions, selection, and live states only.
3. **Consistent vocabulary everywhere.** Same buttons, same chips, same toolbar anatomy on every page — an operator never relearns a screen.
4. **Content first.** Logos, names, statuses are the loudest things on screen; chrome recedes.
5. **Fast feedback.** Skeletons while loading, immediate hover/press states, 150–250ms transitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast against its surface. Focus-visible rings on every interactive element. Touch targets ≥ 40px on mobile. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No color-only status signals — always pair with icon or label.
